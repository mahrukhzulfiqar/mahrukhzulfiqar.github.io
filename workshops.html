Workshop, Future Product Days, Copenhagen, September 2026

The Autonomy Ladder: designing guardrails for agentic AI

A hands-on session that helps product, design and engineering teams decide how much an AI agent should do on its own.

50participants
75 minhands-on
Sold outFuture Product Days 2026
The question
How much should an AI agent be allowed to do on its own, and what has to be true before it gets more autonomy?
How it works
Teams work through realistic agent scenarios as a game, deciding where each one sits on the ladder and which guardrails it needs before it can move up.
Who it's for
Product managers, designers and engineers who are building, or about to build, products with AI agents.
What happened next
Participants asked to buy the game to run with their own teams.
