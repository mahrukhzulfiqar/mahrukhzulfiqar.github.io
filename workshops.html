Workshop at Future Product Days 2026, Copenhagen, 23 September 2026, 15:30 to 16:45

The Autonomy Ladder: how much freedom should you give an AI agent?

Agents are moving from demos into production. But what happens when an agent does exactly what you asked, and not what you intended? The goal isn't maximum autonomy. It's the right autonomy for the risk.

50+participants
75 minhands-on
Sept 2026Future Product Days, Copenhagen
The question
How much should an AI agent be allowed to do on its own, and what has to be true before it gets more autonomy?
How it works
Hands-on, no laptops. Teams build, challenge and pressure-test real agent designs against four questions:
  • What's the cost of this agent being wrong?
  • Is the action reversible?
  • Where does a human need to stay in the loop?
  • Can your guardrails actually hold?
Who it's for
Product managers, designers and engineers who are building, or about to build, products with AI agents.
