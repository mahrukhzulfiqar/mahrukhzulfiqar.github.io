About me

Hi, I'm Mahrukh.

I work between stakeholders and engineering, translating business needs into technical requirements and back again, and back again when the first answer wasn't quite right.

My background in software and data engineering means I can usually tell early whether a plan is realistic, before anyone has committed to it. I like taking things apart to see how they actually work, so I prototype early where it helps. In one case that saved around 300 hours of engineering time by catching the wrong things before they got built.

The road here wasn't straight. I went to school in Denmark, then studied software engineering in Cardiff while Brexit and Covid hit mid-degree. I was rejected by IBM after several rounds of interviews, and told I was too young when I first wanted to move into product management. Each "no" made the next attempt stronger.

Outside my day job I mentor people looking for work in tech in Denmark, reviewing CVs and LinkedIn profiles, and I speak about women in tech and building with AI.
