Live product

GridWise

Tells EV owners in Denmark when to charge for the lowest price and CO2, using Energinet's open data. An AI agent explains the answer; tested code does the maths.

Problem
Prices change every 15 minutes, CO2 shifts with the wind and grid tariffs peak in the evening. The data is public but hard to use.
Key decision
Keep every number out of the language model, so answers are reproducible and testable.
What changed my mind
Research showed many EV owners already charge at night and charger apps already automate it, so I narrowed the target user.
After launch
The data provider added rate limits and the app was blocked within seconds. I redesigned data access the same day.
