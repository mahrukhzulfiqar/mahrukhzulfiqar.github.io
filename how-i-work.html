How I work

Four habits I bring to every product, and the experience they come from.

Prototype before asking for engineering time

At The LEGO Group I built and tested generative AI prototypes with engineering before anything went on the roadmap. Only the strongest use case made it in.

Write the decision down

Every project gets a decision log: what we chose, why, and what we gave up. GridWise has ten.

Let evidence change the plan

Discovery with stakeholders reprioritised half of a platform roadmap before engineering started. On GridWise, research narrowed the target user after launch.

Measure value, not activity

Success is what the user gains, like kroner saved per charging session, not clicks or time in the app.
